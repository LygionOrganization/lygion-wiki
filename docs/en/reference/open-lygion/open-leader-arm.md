# Open Leader Arm

Open Leader Arm is an open robotic-arm structure project. The Wiki currently provides the STEP model for reviewing the mechanism, checking installation relationships, and supporting further design and assembly validation.

## Usage Notes

- Check model units, assembly structure, joint relationships, and part interference in CAD.
- Before building hardware, verify the mounting relationship against the intended actuators, bearings, fasteners, and cable routing.
- Degrees of freedom, travel, payload, BOM, and control architecture are not stated here because they have not yet been confirmed in the source material.
